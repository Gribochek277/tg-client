Rewrite the frame flush to whole-row redraws — fixes scrambled render
The incremental per-cell diff emitted partial runs, which left ghost text,
missing characters and mixed rows once images loaded and the layout
shifted (long multibyte lines + holes + scroll). The flush now re-emits a
COMPLETE row whenever any cell in it changed (holes skipped so Kitty
images stay), with terminal auto-wrap disabled while drawing. A row on
screen is always whole — no ghosts, no dropped characters, stable scroll
in photo-heavy chats. Verified live: scroll up/down through photo chats
stays clean.
