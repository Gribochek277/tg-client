Use Telegram thumbnails for inline images instead of full resolution
Inline photos now download the 'x' (≈800px) thumbnail first, falling back
to 'm' (≈320px) and then the full image — much faster scrolling through
photo-heavy chats. Files are cached as chatId_msgId_x.jpg (thumb) /
chatId_msgId.jpg (full); the cache-hit scan knows both. The 'o' preview
reuses a cached full-res copy if present and fetches it otherwise.
