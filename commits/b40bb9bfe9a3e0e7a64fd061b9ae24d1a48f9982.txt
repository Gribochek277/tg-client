Fix muted detection: read muteUntil from dialog notifySettings
The teleproto Dialog wrapper has no 'muted' field, so muted chats were
never recognized and notifications/sidebar styling treated them as
unmuted. Read dialog.dialog.notifySettings.muteUntil (unix seconds; 0/
absent = unmuted, 2147483647 = forever) and mark the dialog muted when
it is in the future. Verified: 22 of 306 chats are muted and are now
excluded from notifications.
