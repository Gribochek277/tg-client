Respect peer-type mute (mute-all channels/groups) in dialog mute state
The official clients expose type-level notify settings
(account.getNotifySettings with InputNotifyBroadcasts/Chats/Users): here
all channels and groups are muted forever. A chat with an unset per-chat
muteUntil inherits the type-level mute; muteUntil=0 is an explicit
per-chat unmute override. Channels vs supergroups are told apart via the
Channel.megagroup flag (supergroups use the chats setting). Result: 158
of 306 chats muted — matches the official client.
